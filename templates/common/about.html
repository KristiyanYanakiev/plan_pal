About PlanPal

PlanPal is a social planning web application designed for a single friend group. It allows friends to:

  • Create and manage friend profiles
  • Propose activities and events
  • Vote on which proposals to participate in
  • Track upcoming events in a shared space

All friends, proposals, and votes exist in a shared space, and there is no authentication. The goal is to provide a simple and realistic tool for small communities to plan activities together without login or accounts.

PlanPal follows the principle of simplicity while providing meaningful functionality: proposals can be created, updated, confirmed, or cancelled; friends can vote; and the status of upcoming plans is clearly displayed.
