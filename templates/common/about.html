About PlanPal

PlanPal is a social planning web application designed for small groups of friends. Easily propose activities, vote on plans, and keep track of upcoming events — all without the need for authentication.

Key Features

Friends Management

Each member of the social group is represented as a Friend object in the system. Friends can participate in proposals, vote on activities, and track upcoming plans — all without requiring user authentication.

Proposals

Create, update, and manage activity proposals. Track their status: Proposed, Confirmed, Upcoming, Past, or Cancelled.

Voting

Friends can vote Yes or No for any proposal. Votes determine attendees and help plan activities efficiently.
